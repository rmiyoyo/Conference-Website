Hello, sharing world!

InnovationX Commons Africa Summit 2027

An event for openness, sharing and change through collaboration, with people from more than 80 countries, held in Nairobi.

When
Thursday 8 to Friday 9 July 2027
Add to your calendar
Where
Kenyatta International Conference Centre, Nairobi

Main program

  1. Lecture

    Speakers from across the continent on innovation and technology in Africa.

  2. Exhibition

    Work built on open patents by innovators, hobbyists and hackers.

  3. Forum

    Put your questions and opinions to experts and leaders in the tech industry.

  4. Workshop

    Make something yourself with open source tools instead of only watching.

  5. Ignite

    Meet attendees and supporters from across the continent, and stay in touch after the summit.

Featured speakers

  • James Smith

    Chief Technology Officer, Innovate Tech

    James leads a team of engineers and researchers building new products. He has spent over ten years in software development.

  • Sarah Johnson

    Cybersecurity Expert, SecureNet

    Sarah protects organisations from cyber threats and speaks at industry conferences about good security practice.

  • Michael Lee

    Data Scientist, DataWise

    Michael works on machine learning and analytics, and on getting businesses to trust what their data says.

  • Emily Chen

    Product Manager, Tech Innovations

    Emily has a background in product management and user experience design, and cares about products people actually use.

  • Robert Brown

    Blockchain Developer, Crypto Solutions

    Robert builds systems for secure transactions and has worked on several large blockchain projects.

  • Linda Garcia

    AI Researcher, Future AI Labs

    Linda develops AI methods for hard problems and takes part in work on responsible AI.

Getting there

The summit is at the Kenyatta International Conference Centre (KICC) on Harambee Avenue, in Nairobi Central ward, Starehe constituency, Nairobi County.
